Acronym Glossary

This collection uses a lot of ECMS- and DOE-specific shorthand. If you're new, keep this page open in another tab the first few times you read a guide.

TermWhat it means
ECMSEarly Childhood Management System — the Salesforce platform this entire guide collection covers.
DOE(NYC) Department of Education.
CBOCommunity-Based Organization — a vendor that runs childcare sites under a direct contract with DOE.
FCCNFamily Childcare Network — a vendor that oversees a network of individual Family Childcare (FCC) Providers, rather than running sites itself.
FCCFamily Childcare — the broader program under which FCCNs and their affiliated Providers operate.
GFDCGroup Family Day Care — the license/site type most individual FCC Providers hold.
PSGPermission Set Group — a bundle of permissions assigned to a user. This is the real differentiator between ECMS personas for Internal DOE and FCCN users (see the Administrator Guide). A user typically holds several PSGs at once.
Apex SharingSalesforce's programmatic (Apex-code-driven) way of granting record access, as opposed to standard point-and-click sharing rules. ECMS uses Apex sharing keyed off the ECMS User Functions picklist to control what a CBO vendor Contact can see.
Experience User
(Experience Cloud User)		A Salesforce "portal" login used by external personas (CBO vendor staff, FCCN network staff) to access ECMS through a dedicated customer/partner portal space, instead of logging into the standard internal Salesforce UI the way DOE staff do.
OA / ODOperations Analyst / Operations Director — the two-step internal DOE approval chain most Budgets and Invoices go through. "DOD" (Deputy Operations Director) approves alongside/in place of an OD in some workflows.
SDYSchool Day Year — the standard (non-extended) seat/schedule type.
EDYExtended Day (Year) — an extended-hours seat/schedule type that requires an ACCIS eligibility check on submission.
EDFYThe Birth-to-Two/2K extended-day seat type. Unlike EDY, it does not require an ACCIS check.
BTFBirth-To-Five — a Contract Type spanning infant through Pre-K seats.
HS-CTLHead Start / Head Start-CTL — a seat type tied to the federal Head Start program.
LPTLegacy Payment Terms — an older contract structure with its own distinct invoicing schedule, covered separately in the Invoice Creation & Payment guide/tracker.
CRChange Request — e.g. an Enrollment Change Request, used to change a Class, Seat Type, Program Type, or to request a Discharge after an enrollment is already Active.
RFP / RFIRequest for Proposal / Request for Information — procurement opportunities tracked in the Provider Procurement guide.
FAMISFinancial Accounting Management Information System — DOE's core financial system. ECMS posts approved Budgets/Invoices to FAMIS for payment processing, and pulls back payment status.
ACCISAn external childcare-subsidy eligibility system run by NYC's Social Services agency (not DOE). ECMS checks it for Extended Day (EDY) enrollments before they can be finalized.
LCGMSDOE's master directory of every school, site, and building in the city. ECMS checks it to confirm an enrollment's site actually exists and is open, and to look up accessibility information.
OCFSThe state office that licenses Family Childcare Providers (recorded on a Provider Account as its OCFS License Number).
CDAChild Development Associate — a staff credential tracked on Site Staff records.
OTPSOther-Than-Personal-Service costs (e.g. supplies, rent) — as distinct from staff wages/fringe benefits, on a Budget.
UATUser Acceptance Testing — the environment/phase most of this guide collection's screenshots were captured in.
